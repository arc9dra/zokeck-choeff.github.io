Abstract Collection

Abstract illustrations done without a plan in mind, my consciousness on paper. Using only pen, with no pencils or the possibility of erasing. This is my own take on abstract forms, something that I created for myself when I was younger through my own experimentation. When looking closely some aspects may be recognizable.
